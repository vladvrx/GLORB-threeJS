GLORB runtime content

Game application
The GLORB scene/controller engine and its procedural sky/water shaders were
written for this project. Survival, painting, hub and Temple Dash systems are
project-authored application code. Three.js is a third-party library, not code
claimed as the entrant's own engine work.
The expanded garden, five shrine entrances, UFO Rush, Bubble Rush rhythm game,
Dread Warden, residents, wearable hats and dojo use new project-authored geometry,
rules, controls, effects, dialogue and UI.

Authorized artwork
The entrant confirmed rights to the character and UFO assets, character smiley
artwork and sky image. The entrant also confirmed ownership of the GLORB logo,
provided as example5.png and copied unchanged to assets/glorb-logo.png. These are used as authorized third-party/entrant-supplied
assets, not claimed as newly generated art. Exact shipped files and hashes are
listed in asset-provenance.json.

Generated assets
Island geometry and music were generated in this project. Hub and runner scenery
are generated by the readable application code. Sound effects and water/sky
shading are synthesized by the application.
Bubble Rush's original 168 BPM instrumental is synthesized locally by Web Audio.
The dojo's English wisdom quotes are original project writing. It uses the
same local Outfit font as the rest of the game and no speech service.

Excluded material
The recovered application framework, recovered shaders, old vendor runtime
bundles, inherited site data/dialogue, Gilmer fonts, unused artwork
and old sampled audio are not included or requested by this build.
